    About MooW Games Studio

    MooW Games Studio is an Istanbul-based mobile game development studio focused on casual, hybrid-casual, and puzzle games.

    Our team consists of three co-founders, each actively involved in a different area of development: software development, 2D/3D art, and game and level design. This structure allows us to handle the core stages of development in-house, from initial ideas to playable prototypes.

    Through projects with publishers including Unico Studio, Voodoo, Azur Games, TapNation, and iKame, we have gained experience in game development, iteration, and product improvement.

    We focus on prototyping new game ideas quickly, improving games based on publisher feedback, and taking promising projects toward production.

    Our goal is to build a long-term, exclusive publishing partnership to develop new games together and build a sustainable portfolio.

    Our goal

    Our goal is to move beyond project-by-project (PPP) agreements and build a long-term, exclusive publishing partnership.

    We believe our team's production capacity, experience across different puzzle mechanics, and flexible workflow make us well suited to an ongoing development model.

    Since we haven't previously worked under an exclusive publishing agreement, we'd welcome the opportunity to learn more about how you structure these partnerships.

    In particular, we'd like to understand your approach to:

    • Commercial model
    • Development funding
    • Revenue sharing
    • IP ownership
    • Publishing rights
    • Production commitments

    Ultimately, we're looking for a structure that works well for both sides and supports a productive, sustainable, long-term partnership.
